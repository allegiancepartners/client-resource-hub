NYC tax context

NYC founders pay federal, NY State and NYC city tax on exit proceeds — a combined blended rate of approximately 38.6%. This is meaningfully higher than neighboring states and makes the QSBS exclusion and post-exit trust planning especially valuable. A grantor trust — where the founder pays the trust's income tax personally — is particularly compelling at this rate, since it effectively becomes an additional tax-free gift to the trust each year.

NY estate tax — the cliff effect

New York's estate tax exemption is approximately $7.16M — but with a cliff effect: if an estate exceeds 105% of the exemption, the entire estate is taxed, not just the excess. On top of the federal estate tax of 40%, NYS can add up to a further 16% — a combined rate of up to 50%+ at death before anything reaches the next generation. This makes keeping assets outside the taxable estate — via a Dynasty Trust and PPLI structure — especially important for NYC-based founders.
After an exit, Dynasty Trust proceeds can be deployed into a Private Placement Life Insurance policy — either FCV (Frozen Cash Value) or ZCV (Zero Cash Value). Both produce permanently tax-exempt investment growth under current US law. Held within an Irrevocable Trust, the assets also sit completely outside the taxable estate — so the full compounded value passes to the next generation free of both income tax and estate tax. At NYC's 38.6% combined rate, the income tax saving alone is significant. Adjust the illustrative assumptions below.
